## 📌 Executive Summary
This repository contains an audit-ready, enterprise-grade corporate sustainability and decarbonization analytics model built for **Godrej Consumer Products Ltd.** 

The model maps **100+ SEBI BRSR (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting) Principle 6 environmental disclosures** across a 4-year analytical baseline (**FY 2022-23 to FY 2025-26**). It provides deep performance visibility across operational Energy Intensity, Scope 1–3 Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Decarbonization Trajectories, Water Stewardship, and Waste Circularity.

## 📊 Key Highlights & Operational Impact Metrics

* 📉 **Scope 1 Decarbonization:** Achieved a **62.7% reduction in direct emissions** in FY 2025-26 (dropping from 29,544 MtCO₂e down to 11,018 MtCO₂e), driven primarily by biomass boiler fuel substitution.
* ⚡ **Energy Efficiency & Transition:** Modeled **3.78 Million kWh** of verified energy savings across plant-level projects, increasing renewable energy share to **63.2%** of total energy consumption in FY 2025-26.
* ♻️ **Circular Economy Leadership:** Attained a **98.08% waste recovery rate** in FY 2025-26, recycling/recovering **6,189 MT** out of 6,310 MT total generated waste.
* 💧 **Water Conservation & Reclaiming:** Tracked **36,300 KL** of reclaimed and recycled water in FY 2025-26, drastically reducing dependence on local groundwater reserves.

## 🏛️ Excel System Architecture & Data Integrity

The model is constructed using a decoupled 4-layer architecture to guarantee cross-platform stability (Windows/Mac) and prevent data corruption:

| Tab Name | Function & Logic Architecture |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`Executive_Summary`** | **Front-End User Interface:** High-impact executive dashboard featuring KPI scorecard cards, dynamic single-cell year selector dropdown (Cell B6), and synchronized visual trend charts. |
| **`Raw_BRSR_Data`** | **Master Audit Database:** Structured master data housing 117 SEBI BRSR Principle 6 metrics, maintaining restatement audit trails for historical accuracy. |
| **`Calculations`** | **Central Engine:** Formula-driven aggregation engine leveraging dynamic `=SUMIFS()` lookups and `=INDEX(MATCH())` year position routing. Eliminates reliance on unstable macros or pivot caches. |
| **`Initiatives_Tracker`** | **Project Execution Log:** Granular tracking log of 28 plant-level decarbonization, water recycling, and energy efficiency initiatives with localized impact notes. |

## ⚙️ Methodology & Regulatory Compliance Standard

* **Regulatory Framework:** Structured strictly per **SEBI BRSR Core Guidelines (Principle 6 - Respect and Make Efforts to Protect and Restore the Environment)**.
* **GHG Protocol Alignment:** Scope 1 (Direct fuel combustion), Scope 2 (Location-based purchased electricity), and Scope 3 (Value chain emission factors).
* **Formula Engine:** 100% native dynamic lookup engine—built without macro scripts to ensure seamless audit checks by external ESG assurance teams.
